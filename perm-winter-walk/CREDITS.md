## Map data: OpenStreetMap (ODbL 1.0)

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Map data is available under the
[Open Database License](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
The game shows this attribution on screen at all times.

* `game/data/perm/` was produced by `tools/preprocess_osm.py` from OpenStreetMap
  data for central Perm (Esplanade to the Opera and Ballet Theatre). Because the
  build sandbox could not reach the Overpass API or Geofabrik, the bundled copy was
  fetched with `tools/fetch_overture_osm.py` from the Overture Maps mirror, keeping
  **only records whose sources are all OpenStreetMap** (each keeps its OSM way or
  relation id; all OSM-derived Overture data is itself ODbL). Regenerate it from
  Overpass or a Geofabrik extract at any time; see the README.
* The derived chunk files are a Produced Work / Derivative Database under the ODbL.
  If you redistribute the database files themselves, keep them under the ODbL.
* No Google Maps (or any other proprietary map) data is used anywhere.
* Street lamps, trees and snow banks that OSM does not map are *generated*
  procedurally from the OSM road and park geometry.

## Sounds: generated, CC0 1.0

All sounds were synthesised from scratch by `tools/make_sounds.py` (numpy/scipy, no
recorded samples), and are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `game/audio/footsteps/snow_step_00..07.wav` | footsteps in cold dry snow, walking (8 variants) |
| `game/audio/footsteps/snow_run_00..03.wav` | heavier footsteps for running (4 variants) |
| `game/audio/wind_loop.ogg` | 60 s seamless soft wind loop, stereo |

### Swapping in recorded CC0 samples

freesound.org could not be reached from the build sandbox, so no recordings were
downloaded. To use field recordings instead:

1. On freesound.org, search e.g. `snow footsteps` / `snow crunch` and `winter wind`,
   and filter by licence **Creative Commons 0**.
2. Cut single footfalls (about 0.3 s each, 8 or more) and export them as mono WAV.
3. Replace the files above, keeping the names (or edit the lists at the top of
   `game/scripts/fx/footsteps.gd`).
4. Add each sound's title, author, URL and licence to this file. CC0 does not
   require credit, but keeping provenance here makes the licence auditable.

## Textures: generated, CC0 1.0

`game/textures/*.png` (snow noise, snow normal map, boot print, spruce needles and
their normal map, hanging spruce twigs) were generated by `tools/make_textures.py` and
are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

`game/textures/shop_signs.png` holds generic Russian shop words (ПРОДУКТЫ, АПТЕКА,
КАФЕ, …; no brand names), rendered by `tools/make_textures.py` with the **DejaVu Sans
Bold** font (Bitstream Vera licence with DejaVu changes in the public domain; free to
use, and only a raster image of the text is included, not the font).

Window patterns, rooms behind windows, lamp light, sky and snowflakes are drawn by
shaders: no further image assets.
